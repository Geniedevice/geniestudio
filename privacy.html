privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026.

GenieStudio is a fan-made YouTube channel that posts short, English-subtitled clips of Korean VTubers. This page explains what data this website and our upload tool use.

This website

The site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. Your browser reads your time zone only to show when the next clip goes up; that never leaves your device. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typeface from Google Fonts; both receive your IP address like any web request.

GenieStudio Shorts Uploader

We use a small desktop tool, which only the channel owner runs on their own computer, to publish our clips to our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to anyone else. With the owner’s Google sign-in it:

  • uploads our own videos and sets their titles, descriptions, translations and publish times;
  • adds caption tracks to our own videos;
  • reads the view, like and comment counts and comment times of our own videos, to choose better posting times.

The sign-in token and these numbers are stored only on the owner’s computer. They are not sold, shared or used for advertising, and no data about other YouTube users is collected beyond the public comment times on our own videos. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube’s own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
